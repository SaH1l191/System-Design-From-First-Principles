# Architecture Patterns

> How you organize your system determines how it scales, how it fails, and how your team works.

The big architectural decisions. Monolith vs microservices isn't a binary choice — it's a spectrum, and understanding where you are on that spectrum is what matters.

## The Decision Framework

Ask yourself:
1. **Team size** — can your team own separate services?
2. **Domain complexity** — is the business logic separable?
3. **Scale requirements** — do different parts need different scaling?
4. **Operational maturity** — can you run distributed systems?

Start monolith. Split when the pain of staying together exceeds the pain of splitting.
