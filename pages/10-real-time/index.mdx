# Real-Time & Communication

> When request-response isn't fast enough.

Everything you need to know about real-time communication, video streaming, and presence systems.

---

## Topics

| Chapter | What It Covers |
|---------|---------------|

---

## The Real-Time Spectrum

| Technique | Direction | Latency | Complexity |
|-----------|-----------|---------|------------|
| Short Polling | Client → Server | High | Low |
| Long Polling | Client ↔ Server | Medium | Low |
| SSE | Server → Client | Low | Medium |
| WebSockets | Bidirectional | Very Low | High |
| Socket.IO | Bidirectional | Low | Medium |

Pick the simplest one that meets your latency requirements.
